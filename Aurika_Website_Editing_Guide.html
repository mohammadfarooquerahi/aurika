01 Accessing WordPress Admin

To manage your store, log in to your store's administration dashboard:

URL: https://aurika.online/wp-admin
  1. Enter your Username / Email Address and Password.
  2. Click Log In to reach the main WordPress Dashboard.
  3. On the left-side vertical black menu bar, you will find all management sections (Appearance, Products, WooCommerce, Pages, Orders).

02 The Theme Customizer: Your Primary Control Panel

Virtually all text, images, titles, headings, reviews, and store rules can be modified with a live visual preview in the Customizer:

Location: WP Admin → Appearance → Customize → Aurika Theme Settings

When you open the Customizer, a preview of your website appears on the right, and the settings panel is on the left. Any change you type immediately updates the preview on the right in real time!

Golden Rule: After making edits in the Customizer, always click the blue "Publish" button at the very top of the left sidebar to save your changes to the live site.

17 Step-by-Step: Adding Scented Candles

Location: WP Admin → Products → Add New

Adding a luxury scented candle takes less than 2 minutes. The theme automatically configures the 1-Pack, 2-Pack (10% Off), and 3-Pack (15% Off) interactive cards for customers!

  1. Product Name: Enter the candle's name (e.g. Amber & Vanilla Scented Candle).
  2. Main Description Box: Write the sensory description and fragrance notes:
    • Top Notes: Bergamot, Warm Amber.
    • Heart Notes: French Vanilla, Tonka Bean.
    • Base Notes: Sandalwood, White Musk.
    Note: This text automatically populates the first "About The Fragrance" accordion on the product page!
  3. Product Short Description: Enter a 1-line luxury teaser (e.g. Handcrafted organic soy candle with soothing vanilla notes). This displays as the subtitle under the main price.
  4. Product Data Box:
    • Product Type: Keep as "Simple product".
    • Regular Price (Rs.): Enter single candle price (e.g. 1299). The theme's bundle math automatically calculates:
      • 1-Pack = Rs. 1,299
      • 2-Pack = Rs. 2,338 (10% Off)
      • 3-Pack = Rs. 3,312 (15% Off)
  5. Product Image (Right Column): Click "Set product image" to upload your primary aesthetic photo (minimum 1000 × 1000px square).
  6. Product Gallery: Upload 2–4 photos (close-up of wax, lifestyle in room, packaging unboxing).
  7. Product Categories (Right Column): Check "Scented Candles".
  8. Click the blue "Publish" button. Your candle is live with pack discounts and sticky add-to-cart!

18 Step-by-Step: Adding DIY Art Kits

Location: WP Admin → Products → Add New

DIY Art Kits feature a custom Small Kit (4 Toys) vs. Big Kit (8 Toys) selector with paints & brush badges:

  1. Product Name: Must include the word "DIY" or "Art Kit" in the title (e.g. Safari Animals DIY Art Kit).
  2. Main Description: List the figures included (e.g. Lion, Elephant, Giraffe, Zebra), paint pots, brush, and non-toxic certificate.
  3. Product Data: Set Regular Price to 299 (or your base kit price). The theme's DIY selector will automatically display:
    • Small Kit (4 Toys): Rs. 299
    • Big Kit (8 Toys): Rs. 599 (Best Value Badge)
    (Both prices can be changed anytime in Appearance → Customize → 💰 Pricing & Shipping Rules).
  4. Category: Check "DIY Art Kits".
  5. Product Image: Upload a colorful photo showing the concrete toys, paints, and brush together.
  6. Click Publish.

19 Managing Customer Orders & Cash on Delivery (COD)

Location: WP Admin → WooCommerce → Orders

When a customer completes checkout on Aurika, their order instantly appears under WooCommerce → Orders.

  1. Review New Orders: New orders have status "Processing" (for prepaid) or "On Hold" / "Processing" (for Cash on Delivery).
  2. View Customer Details: Click the customer's name or Order ID to view:
    • Customer Phone Number (for WhatsApp confirmation).
    • Full Delivery Address & City.
    • Selected Pack Size (e.g. 2 Pack or Big Kit – 8 Concrete Toys).
    • Total Amount + Shipping Fee (Free if ≥ Rs. 5,000, or Rs. 350 flat).
  3. WhatsApp Order Confirmation: Before dispatching COD parcels, send a quick confirmation WhatsApp message to the customer to ensure address accuracy and prevent courier return fees.
  4. Update Order Status:
    • When parcel is handed to the courier (Trax, Leopard, PostEx, TCS), change status to "Completed".
    • An automated dispatch email is sent to the customer automatically!

20 Approving Reviews & LiteSpeed Cache Purging

Location: WP Admin → Products → Reviews & LiteSpeed Cache Toolbar
Task Instructions
Customer Reviews Approval When customers submit a review on a product page, it awaits moderation in Products → Reviews. Hover over the review and click "Approve". The 5-star rating and comment will appear publicly immediately!
Purging LiteSpeed Cache If you change a price, picture, or banner in the Customizer and it doesn't show right away on your phone or incognito tab, LiteSpeed is serving the cached HTML. In the top black admin bar, hover over the green LiteSpeed diamond icon and click "Purge All". Then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl + F5).
Inventory Low-Stock Alerts Set email alerts for low inventory under WooCommerce → Settings → Products → Inventory so you never run out of raw natural wax or kit supplies during sales.
